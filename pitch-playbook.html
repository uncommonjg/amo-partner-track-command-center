PA Programs — Existing Customer
PA Programs · Existing Customer
Upsell to Clinical Core track
School has been booking PA rotations with AMO — piecemeal or per-slot. May be using 1–2 rotations per year in a specific geography.
Opening Hook
"You've been booking [specialty] in [geography] for [X] years. We want to make sure you never lose that — and we want to show you what's around it that you're not using."
Core Message
After working with over 65 partner programs, the schools with the best student outcomes — and the least coordinator headaches — are the ones who plan their geography for the full academic year. That's Clinical Core. Lock in what you already have. Expand around it in the same geography. Students don't relocate mid-year. You don't scramble every fall when the calendar opens.
Value Props
  • Guaranteed access — your rotation is reserved before the calendar opens to anyone else
  • Geographic continuity — wrap additional specialties around your anchor rotation in the same hub
  • Students stay in one city — housing, community, better clinical experience
  • Predictable cost, predictable supply — one contract, one contact, one academic year plan
  • 45-day replacement SLA in writing — preceptor drops out, we cover it
Objection Flips
"We can't commit to a full year."
You're already committing every year — you're just doing it reactively. Clinical Core makes that commitment explicit and gives you guaranteed access in return.
"We have an internal team that manages this."
Your internal team can't guarantee specialty depth across a full geography or provide a replacement within 45 days in writing. What happens when your preceptor pulls out three weeks before the rotation?
"We've had bad experiences with placement vendors."
We're not a placement vendor. We're a clinical infrastructure partner. [Reference a nearby AMO partner program here.] That program uses us to run their entire Ohio cohort — ask them.
Call to Action
20 minutes with your clinical coordinator or program director to map what your geographic hub would look like for AY 2027 — before planning locks up.
PA Programs — Net New
PA Programs · Net New
Cold intro — hub in their geography
No prior relationship with AMO or minimal historical touchpoint. Don't talk pricing on first contact. Lead with the pain, reference the hub, book the call.
Opening Hook
"PA programs in [State] are facing a 40%+ preceptor shortage in [specialty]. ARC-PA reviewers are flagging it. We built a hub in [geography] to solve exactly that — and we have capacity for your cohort in AY 2027."
Core Message
AMO has built clinical hub markets specifically for PA programs — verified, credentialed preceptors across all ARC-PA required rotation types, in geographic clusters so students aren't bouncing between cities. We work with [X] PA programs nationally. Programs that come to us typically come because they've lost a preceptor last-minute or are in a specialty they can't find on their own. We'd rather talk before that moment happens.
Value Props
  • Ready-built inventory in their geography — no cold-start, no building from scratch
  • Specialty mix covers all ARC-PA required rotation types
  • Academic year planning support — we map your cohort against available inventory before calendar opens
  • 45-day replacement SLA in writing
  • Programs nearby that can speak to the experience (use ARC-PA annotated list for warm references)
Objection Flips
"We handle our clinical placements internally."
Most programs said that before their preceptor base started contracting. We're not replacing your process — we're the safety net for the specialties your internal team can't consistently source.
"We haven't heard of AMO."
That's exactly what this call is for. We work with [nearby program name]. I can connect you with their clinical director if it's helpful to hear it from a peer program.
Call to Action
20-minute call with [Nic R / Nick V] to see what AMO's [geography] hub looks like for your specialty mix and AY 2027 cohort size. No commitment — just a map of what's available.
DO Programs — Existing Customer
DO Programs · Existing Customer
Upsell to academic year hub commitment
School has done rotation business with AMO — possibly electives, possibly a trial. Relationship exists but isn't structured. They're not on a full track.
Opening Hook
"We've placed [X] of your students in [geography]. Clinical Core is what the next chapter looks like — not just slots, but an academic year plan built around your COCA requirements."
Core Message
DO programs have a different compliance burden than PA programs — COCA inpatient requirements, OMM access, hospital-based tracks. Most DO programs are piecing this together one rotation at a time. Clinical Core gives you a geographic hub with hospital-based tracks locked in for the full academic year. Your students stay in one city. Their continuity improves. Your coordinators aren't rebuilding the plan every semester.
Value Props
  • Hospital-based tracks with inpatient access — covers COCA core rotation requirements
  • Geographic hub — students who stay in one geography for the year get better clinical continuity and outcomes
  • Academic year reserved — slots secured before any other program can access them
  • Elective rotation options wrapped around core tracks in the same hub
  • One contract, one point of contact for the full cohort
Objection Flips
"We have health system partnerships that cover our sites."
Great — those cover your anchor sites. Clinical Core covers what your health system doesn't: elective subspecialties, geographic flexibility for students not based near your anchor, and the backup when your health system contact changes.
"We're not sure this is the right fit for a DO program."
Fair — let us show you the specific track we've built for DO programs in [geography]. It's not a PA product with a DO label. It's built around COCA requirements and hospital access. 20 minutes to walk through the map.
Call to Action
20-minute call to map what an AY 2027 DO hub schedule would look like for your cohort in [geography] — built around your COCA rotation requirements.
DO Programs — Net New
DO Programs · Net New
Cold intro — packaged DO hub inventory
No relationship with AMO. Most likely the OMED scenario. Lead with the hub, make the elective rotation offer the door-opener.
Opening Hook
"There are 40 DO programs nationally. Every one of them has the same elective rotation gaps. We built a hub in [geography] for exactly that — hospital-based tracks and elective rotations in one place, with no school using it yet."
Core Message
AMO has built hub markets specifically for DO programs — hospital-based tracks, inpatient access, elective rotations across key specialties, all in a geographic cluster. For programs that don't have a health system partnership covering a particular region, this is the turnkey solution. We're not asking for a full commitment on first contact — we want to show you what the hub looks like for your cohort size and rotation mix.
Value Props
  • DO-specific track model — built around COCA requirements, not repurposed from PA inventory
  • Geographic hub — students stay in one city, reducing housing and logistics burden
  • Ready now for AY 2027 — no cold-start or preceptor recruitment lag
  • Free elective rotation for one 4th-year student (OMED offer) — gift your program can give to a top student
  • Academic year planning support with guaranteed availability
Objection Flips
"We're tied to [health system] for all our sites."
Tell us where your gaps are. Our hub covers specialties most health systems won't touch — elective subspecialties, programs outside your system's footprint, and OMM-adjacent rotations. We work around your existing partnerships, not against them.
"We've never heard of AMO."
That's what the 20 minutes is for. We work with [DO reference program if any]. We're at OMED — come by the booth or let us grab time while you're here. No pitch, just a map of what we have in your geography.
Call to Action
Booth conversation at OMED → follow-up 20-minute call with Nick V within the next 2 weeks. We'll map out what the DO hub looks like for your geography before AY 2027 planning locks up.
