Diagnostic Hypotheses
🔴
Critical Joel Micaela
H1 · Google Ads Conversion Architecture Failure
AMO's Google campaigns were optimizing toward sign-ups as the conversion event, not bookings. The reported blended CPA of $6.38 was cost-per-signup. The actual cost-per-booking was $1,100.24. Meanwhile, paid search traffic dropped -42% YTD, suggesting Google's algorithm learned from the misaligned signal and degraded campaign quality over time. Google leads are up 21× in 2026 (from Marlo's data) while bookings from that channel barely moved.
Google CPB: $1,100 actual vs $6.38 reported Paid Search traffic -42% YTD Google leads +21× YTD; bookings flat Source: Booking Leak Diagnosis PDF (Joel) · Marketing Reporting Package (Micaela)
🔴
Critical Micaela
H2 · Email Database Decay and Delivery Collapse
HubSpot health score fell from 57 (OK) in Oct 2025 to 27 (Poor) in Oct 2026, a 53% drop in 12 months. Contributing events: mass outreach sends with insufficient hygiene, a portal merge that combined B2B and B2C contacts without suppression lists, and a single Oct 2026 send generating 2,947 hard bounces. Email was a top-4 acquisition channel (30,064 new users in 2024). Its collapse directly kills re-engagement of the 44K dormant profiles who never applied. That is the exact cohort with the highest latent conversion potential.
Health score 57 → 27 in 12 months 2,947 hard bounces on Oct 7–9 send Open rate 8.2% (target 12.8%) Hard bounce rate 1.1% (target 0.5%) Source: Marketing Reporting Package, Email Health tab (Micaela)
🟠
High Marlo Ryan
H3 · B2B2C Partnership Lead Quality Collapse
Partnership leads are up 23.5% YTD; bookings are down 28.8%. The lead→application rate for partnerships fell from 27.8% (2025 YTD) to 14.6% (2026 YTD). Marlo's log-decomposition bridge isolates -168 bookings directly attributable to this rate deterioration, separate from volume effects. Most likely cause: partner organizations are sending earlier-stage, less-qualified students (pre-USMLE, non-visa-eligible, or India-concentrated) whose interest in rotations exists but whose ability to actually book does not.
Partnership leads +23.5%; bookings -28.8% YTD Lead→App rate 27.8% (2025) → 14.6% (2026) Log-decomp bridge: -168 bookings from rate alone Source: AMO IMG Monthly Comparison (Marlo) · High-Value Leads (Ryan)
🟠
High Marlo GA Data
H4 · Organic Search Infrastructure Degradation
Organic search traffic is down -34% YTD. Organic leads fell -47% in H1 2026 vs H1 2025. Website/organic applications fell -57% over the same period. This is compounding degradation: traffic loss plus quality loss within remaining traffic. Likely causes: Google's Helpful Content Update (2024), March/November 2024 core algorithm updates, possible technical site issues, or link equity decay. Organic historically carried zero marginal cost-per-acquisition. Its collapse amplifies every paid cost metric and has no easy or fast fix.
Organic Search traffic -34% YTD Organic leads -47% H1 2026 Organic applications -57% H1 2026 Organic new users 52.8K (2024) → 28.9K (2025 FY) Source: Monthly Comparison (Marlo) · GA4 Cohorts Export
🔵
Medium · Investigating Ryan Aishlin GA Data
H5 · India Visa Policy Chilling Effect
India is AMO's #1 country by traffic (359K active users in 2025) with a 38% engagement rate (lowest of any top-5 country) and -63% payment decline YTD. India USMLE signups fell -35% YTD and the booking rate for India USMLE leads is only 1.98% vs Korea's 10.1% for the identical signal profile. Korea and India have comparable USMLE interest but fundamentally different US visa accessibility. The pattern is consistent with an external chilling effect: students who want rotations but perceive them as inaccessible due to J-1/B-1 visa difficulty, not undesirable. If confirmed, this cannot be solved by marketing investment alone. It requires product-level intervention (visa guidance, sponsorship support, or alternative rotation markets).
India payments -63% YTD India engagement rate 38% (lowest top-5) India USMLE booking rate 1.98% vs Korea 10.1% India USMLE signups -35% YTD Source: High-Value Leads (Ryan) · Application Data (Aishlin) · GA4 Demographics CSV
These hypotheses are not independent. Google Ads failure → underreported CPB → continued misallocated investment. Email decay → reduced re-engagement of 44K dormant profiles. Organic collapse → heavier paid dependence at inflated true cost. India visa chilling → partnership quality collapse (MSAI-India and India-heavy partners). Each hypothesis reinforces the others. The result: a measurement dashboard that showed health while the underlying system deteriorated across every compoundable vector simultaneously.
