Why programs come to us

The preceptor burden is real, and it compounds.

  • ARC-PA requires documented clinical placement capacity. Relying on individual coordinator outreach creates compliance exposure when placements fall through.
  • Sourcing preceptors semester-by-semester consumes coordinator capacity that should go to students, not search.
  • Programs that can guarantee placement can grow enrollment. Programs that can't are capped, or risk compliance exposure.
  • Specialty gaps delay students, disrupt scheduling, and surface in accreditation reviews.
How Clinical Core works

A hub in your market. Managed by AMO.

Geography-first placement

Your students rotate within your market, without relocating. Preceptors are recruited, credentialed, and organized around your geographic footprint.

Specialty matching

AMO matches students to preceptors by specialty, not just availability. Core PA rotation types covered across the hub.

Academic-year commitment

Lock in rotation blocks for the full academic year. Programs know their placement capacity before the year starts, with no last-minute search.

ARC-PA ready documentation

AMO provides placement records and preceptor credentials in the format accreditation reviewers expect.
