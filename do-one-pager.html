The DO placement challenge

Regional programs need regional preceptors. They're harder to find than most realize.

  • DO students are expected to stay close to their program's base. Rotations that require significant travel create attrition risk and student hardship.
  • Building a local preceptor network from scratch takes years. Maintaining it takes more. New and growing programs are especially exposed.
  • COCA requires documented placement capacity. Specialty gaps that surface during accreditation review create timelines programs can't afford.
  • At scale, managing rotation logistics internally is a coordinator tax with compounding overhead as class size grows.
