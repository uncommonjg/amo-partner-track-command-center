PA core rotations only. Every number on this page uses preceptor listings that accept Physician Assistant students (in-person) in the seven core rotations: family medicine, internal medicine (including hospitalist), pediatrics, OB/GYN (including women's health and maternal-fetal medicine), general surgery, psychiatry (including child and adolescent) and emergency medicine. Electives are left out. Booked blocks and schools count PA rotations only. Open seats subtract every rotation placed on a PA listing, so a seat is never shown as open when someone else holds it. Where a preceptor has separate PA and DO/MD listings, only the PA listing is counted.

Partner school view. Pick a school to see its PA core blocks in its own current and next clinical year, starting from the school's clinical year start month. Start months come from the AY Start property on the school's HubSpot company record (Refresh HubSpot activity pulls the latest). Where AY Start is blank, the page uses a month set on this page, then the month most of the school's students start their first AMO rotation; fill in AY Start in HubSpot to make it permanent. Blocks are numbered from the clinical year start using the school's usual block length. Recommendations supplement the core rotations the school already books through AMO. For next year, each rotation's target is how many fewer blocks the school has booked than in its current clinical year, and clusters are ranked by how many of those they can cover. For the current year, clusters are ranked by open seats in those rotations. Only months the school rotates in (from its current clinical year pattern) count, clusters it already uses come first, and critical rotations it doesn't book yet are shown as add-ons but not scored.

Price and credits. List price is the preceptor's 4-week list price in the AMO platform. PA credit value comes from the PA price ceiling grid (up to $1,999 = 1 credit; $2,000 to $2,499 = 1.25; $2,500 to $2,999 = 1.5; $3,000 to $3,498 = 1.75; $3,499 to $3,998 = 2). Preceptors priced above $3,998 show “above grid.” Open value multiplies open blocks by the list price and by the credit value.

Which preceptors count. Markets follow the AMO B2B Eligibility Standard: live PA listings, plus unlisted (hidden) PA listings whose HubSpot program deal is in Resale/School (Hidden) or Live. Hidden listings in any other stage are left out. Core buckets follow the standard: internal medicine includes hospitalist, geriatric and critical care; OB/GYN includes women's health and maternal-fetal medicine but not gynecology-only; general surgery includes bariatric. The NJCC/RICC captive network is excluded because it is held for one school. Refresh HubSpot activity to update deal stages.

How markets are defined. A cluster is any 50-mile radius around a bookable PA preceptor that holds at least five of the seven core specialties (family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, OB/GYN, general surgery, psychiatry, emergency medicine). Clusters are drawn greedily from the densest point outward so no preceptor counts in two clusters. Clinical hubs are preceptors tagged Hub-… in the AMO platform, listed or not; a hub preceptor may also sit inside a cluster. The hub school is the school with the most hub bookings on file since January 2026. Hub availability uses each preceptor's offered seats, offered months and blocked months; a month counts as used when a rotation covers at least 14 days of it.

Who to sell next. Each market lists three groups of schools. Schools booked here are the ones with blocks in the selected period or later. Win back lists partner schools that booked here earlier (booking data starts January 2026) but have nothing in the selected period or later. Cross sell lists PA partners that book through AMO elsewhere in rotations this market offers, ranked by how many of their rotations it covers; their AY start comes from HubSpot.

Reservation activity. Each market lists rotations requested (the application was submitted) or reserved in the 7 days before the last data pull, on that market's preceptors. All markets also lists partner school (B2B) requests and reservations on preceptors outside every cluster and hub. Cancelled, refunded, switched and transferred rotations are left out. Refresh bookings pulls the latest 7 days.

Blocks. One booked rotation is one block. Confirmed = Reserved, Enrolled or Pending-Booked. In process = Accepted, Pre-Approved or Pending. Use the period switch at the top to move every tab between January to December 2026, July 2026 to June 2027, January to December 2027 and July 2027 to June 2028. A block belongs to the period its rotation starts in, so the calendar years and academic years overlap. Months that have already passed are greyed out and never counted as open. Estimated fill covers the critical specialties only (pediatrics, OB/GYN, general surgery and emergency medicine): blocks booked in those specialties divided by 12 four-week blocks per live preceptor in them per year. It is a planning guide, not a hard capacity.
